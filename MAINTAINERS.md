# Maintainer Policy

## Ownership and Permissions
- Maintainers review architecture-affecting changes.
- Direct pushes to protected branches are disallowed.

## Change Control
- Version and changelog updates are required for user-facing changes.
- Dependency changes must follow `docs/dependency_policy.md`.

## Compliance Notes
- Reject logging patterns that may leak PII.
- Ensure legal/privacy/KYC docs remain present and discoverable.
